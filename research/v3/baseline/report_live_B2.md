### B. Linguistic, Grammatical, and Lexical Relationships

1. **`מִכְשׁוֹל` (H4383) & `מוֹקֵשׁ` (H4170) → `πρόσκομμα` (G4348) & `σκάνδαλον` (G4625):**
   - MCP `research (lxx translations)` confirms that in the BCV/Swete LXX corpus, `מִכְשׁוֹל` (H4383) is attested with `πρόσκομμα` (8× in Swete), `προσκοπή` (8×), and `σκάνδαλον` (3× in BCV/Swete, e.g., Lev 19:14, Ps 118[119]:165), while `σκάνδαλον` (G4625) renders `מוֹקֵשׁ` (H4170, 5×) and `מִכְשׁוֹל` (H4383, 3×).
   - Crucially, in **Isa 8:14**, Swete LXX uses `λίθου προσκόμματι` for `אֶבֶן נֶגֶף` (actually aligning `πρόσκομμα` with `נֶגֶף` [H5063], even though `נֶגֶף` elsewhere means "plague/blow," e.g., `πληγή` in Exod 12:13) and uses `πέτρας πτώματι` for `צוּר מִכְשׁוֹל`. Both **Rom 9:33** and **1 Pet 2:8** retain `λίθος/λίθον προσκόμματος` from the Isa 8:14 Greek tradition, yet replace `πτώματι` with `πέτρα(ν) σκανδάλου`—a more idiomatic Greek rendering of `צוּר מִכְשׁוֹל` (and echoing `מוֹקֵשׁ` later in Isa 8:14).
2. **`חוּשׁ` (H2363A, "to hasten") → `καταισχύνω` (G2617):**
   - MCP `research (lxx translations)` shows `H2363A` (`חוּשׁ`, "to hasten"; distinct from homograph `H2363B` "to enjoy") is rendered in Swete LXX by `προστρέχω` (7×), `κατασπεύδω` (2×), `σπεύδω` (1×), and `ταχύνω` (1×)—**never** `καταισχύνω`. Conversely, `get_Passage` lexicon data for `G2617` (`καταισχύνω`) links it primarily to `בּוֹשׁ` (H0954, 6×) and `קָלוֹן`/`קָלָה` (H7036/H7034). Thus, LXX Isa 28:16's `οὐ μὴ καταισχυνθῇ` is an interpretive translation (or reflects a `יבוש`-type reading/semantic extension from "panic/flee in haste" to "be put to shame"), and **Rom 9:33, Rom 10:11, and 1 Pet 2:6 all depend on the LXX** against the MT here.
3. **`אָמַן` (H0539) & `אֱמוּנָה` (H0530) → `πιστεύω` (G4100) & `πίστις` (G4102):**
   - MCP `lxx` bridges show `H0539` (`אָמַן`, Hiphil `הַמַּאֲמִין` in Isa 28:16) corresponds to `πιστεύω` (G4100, 31× in BCV) and `πιστός` (G4103, 27×), while `H0530` (`אֱמוּנָה`, "faithfulness/steadfastness" in Hab 2:4) is split evenly in BCV LXX between `ἀλήθεια` (G0225, 16×) and `πίστις` (G4102, 16×).
   - This lexical bridge enabled Paul in **Rom 9:32–33** to connect `πίστις` (`ἐκ πίστεως`) with Isa 28:16 (`ὁ πιστεύων`), and in **Rom 1:17** / **Gal 3:11** to read Hab 2:4's `ἐκ πίστεως` without the 3ms/1cs possessive pronoun (`בֶּאֱמוּנָתוֹ` / `μου`), allowing `ἐκ πίστεως` to modify either `ὁ δίκαιος` or `ζήσεται` in the Pauline contrast with `ἐκ νόμου` / `ἐξ ἔργων`.
4. **`עָפַל` (H6075) → `ὑποστέλλω` (G5288):**
   - `H6075` is a rare root (2 verses: `עָפַל` Pual `עֻפְּלָה` in Hab 2:4, "swollen/puffed up," and Hiphil `וַיַּעְפִּלוּ` in Num 14:44, rendered in LXX Num 14:44 by `διαβιασάμενοι`, "forcing their way presumptuously"). LXX Hab 2:4's `ἐὰν ὑποστείληται` ("if he shrinks back/draws back") is lexically distant from MT `עֻפְּלָה` (possibly reading a different Vorlage such as `עֻלְּפָה` "faint/collapse" or interpreting arrogance as cowardly withdrawal). Hebrews 10:38–39 (`ὑποστείληται` / `ὑποστολῆς`) builds its entire pastoral warning against apostasy on this LXX-specific lexical choice (`G5288` occurs only 4× in the NT: Acts 20:20, 27; Gal 2:12; Heb 10:38).
5. **Grammar & Case Shifts:**
   - **Ps 118:22 (`λίθον` → `λίθος`):** LXX Ps 117:22 exhibits inverse relative-clause attraction (`λίθον ὃν ἀπεδοκίμασαν...`, retained in Matt 21:42, Mark 12:10, Luke 20:17). 1 Peter 2:7 breaks this attraction (`λίθος ὃν ἀπεδοκίμασαν...`) so that `λίθος` stands in nominative parallel with `λίθος προσκόμματος` (v. 8) and contrasts with `ἡ τιμή` (v. 7a).
   - **Hab 2:3 (`ἐρχόμενος` → `ὁ ἐρχόμενος`):** LXX renders Hebrew infinitive absolute `בֹּא יָבֹא` ("it will surely come") with an anarthrous circumstantial participle (`ἐρχόμενος ἥξει`). Hebrews 10:37 inserts the definite article `ὁ` (`ὁ ἐρχόμενος`), transforming an emphatic Hebrew verbal construction about the *vision* into a personal/messianic title ("the Coming One").
   - **Isa 28:16 (`פִּנָּה` H6438 → `ἀκρογωνιαῖος` G0204):** `G0204` (`ἀκρογωνιαῖος`) occurs only twice in the NT—**1 Pet 2:6** (quoting LXX Isa 28:16) and **Eph 2:20** (`ὄντος ἀκρογωνιαίου αὐτοῦ Χριστοῦ Ἰησοῦ`, echoing `θεμέλιος` + `ἀκρογωνιαῖος` from LXX Isa 28:16).

### C. Structural & Rhetorical Parallels / Splicing Techniques

1. **Catchword Linking (*Gezerah Shavah*) Across the Stone Catena:**
   - `get_Connection` directly surfaced the lexical hooks binding the three OT stone texts:
     - `אֶבֶן` (H0068) / `λίθος` (G3037) links **Isa 28:16**, **Isa 8:14**, and **Ps 118:22**.
     - `פִּנָּה` (H6438, 31v) links **Isa 28:16** (`פִּנַּת יִקְרַת`) and **Ps 118:22** (`לְרֹאשׁ פִּנָּה`).
2. **Conflation (Splicing) in Romans 9:33:**
   - Paul uses Isa 28:16 as an outer syntactic envelope (`ἰδοὺ τίθημι ἐν Σιὼν λίθον ... καὶ ὁ πιστεύων ἐπ᾽ αὐτῷ οὐ καταισχυνθήσεται`) and splices Isa 8:14 (`προσκόμματος καὶ πέτραν σκανδάλου`) into the middle `λίθον` slot—transforming the "precious cornerstone" of Isa 28:16 into a "stone of stumbling" in a single composite `καθὼς γέγραπται` citation.
   - Notably, in Rom 9:32 (`προσέκοψαν τῷ λίθῳ τοῦ προσκόμματος`), Paul prepares the catchword `προσέκοψαν` (`G4350`) ↔ `προσκόμματος` (`G4348`) right before the quotation (detected by `get_Passage` literary analysis).
3. **Expansive Tripartite Catena in 1 Peter 2:4–8:**
   - Rather than splicing Isa 28:16 and Isa 8:14 into one sentence as Paul does, 1 Peter 2:4–8 introduces **all three** stone texts sequentially after a programmatic thematic synthesis in v. 4 (`λίθον ζῶντα, ὑπὸ ἀνθρώπων μὲν ἀποδεδοκιμασμένον [Ps 118:22], παρὰ δὲ θεῷ ἐκλεκτὸν ἔντιμον [Isa 28:16]`):
     1. **v. 6:** Isa 28:16 (`διότι περιέχει ἐν γραφῇ...`)
     2. **v. 7:** Ps 118:22 (`ὑμῖν οὖν ἡ τιμὴ τοῖς πιστεύουσιν· ἀπιστοῦσιν δὲ...`)
     3. **v. 8:** Isa 8:14 (`καὶ λίθος προσκόμματος καὶ πέτρα σκανδάλου`), followed by `οἳ προσκόπτουσιν` (`G4350`, mirroring Paul's v. 32 wordplay) and `εἰς ὃ καὶ ἐτέθησαν` (`G5087`, echoing `τίθημι` in v. 6!).
   - Furthermore, `ἔντιμον` (v. 6) generates `ἡ τιμὴ` (v. 7), and `πιστεύων` (v. 6) generates `τοῖς πιστεύουσιν· ἀπιστοῦσιν δέ` (v. 7), weaving the three citations into a tight antithetical diptych (believers vs. unbelievers).
4. **Splicing and Clause-Inversion in Hebrews 10:37–38:**
   - Hebrews 10:37 splices **LXX Isaiah 26:20** (`μικρὸν ὅσον ὅσον`, linked by `ἔτι γάρ` to LXX Hab 2:3a `διότι ἔτι`) onto **LXX Habakkuk 2:3b** (`ὁ ἐρχόμενος ἥξει καὶ οὐ χρονίσει`).
   - Hebrews 10:38 then **inverts the clause order** of Hab 2:4 (putting `2:4b` before `2:4a`) so that the positive promise (`ὁ δὲ δίκαιός μου ἐκ πίστεως ζήσεται`) follows immediately after `ὁ ἐρχόμενος ἥξει`, and the warning (`καὶ ἐὰν ὑποστείληται, οὐκ εὐδοκεῖ ἡ ψυχή μου ἐν αὐτῷ`) stands at the end—creating an immediate bridge into v. 39's rhetorical antithesis (`ὑποστολῆς εἰς ἀπώλειαν ἀλλὰ πίστεως εἰς περιποίησιν ψυχῆς`).
5. **Contextual Echoes in Isaiah 28:14–18 and Isaiah 8:13–15:**
   - `literary_structure` highlights that MT Isa 28:15–18 contrasts the Jerusalem rulers' "covenant with death / shelter of falsehood" (`שֶׁקֶר`, `כָּזָב`, `שׁוֹט שׁוֹטֵף`) with YHWH's laid stone (`יִסַּד ... מוּסָד`).
   - In Isa 8:13–15, MT's `וְכָשְׁלוּ בָם רַבִּים וְנָפְלוּ וְנִשְׁבָּרוּ` ("and many will stumble on them and fall and be broken", root `כשל` linking `מִכְשׁוֹל` in 8:14 to `וְכָשְׁלוּ` in 8:15) stands behind the judgment motif in Luke 20:18 / Matt 21:44 (`ὁ πεσὼν ἐπὶ τὸν λίθον τοῦτον συνθλασθήσεται`) appended right after Ps 118:22.

### D. Interpretive Hypotheses (Explicitly Separated from Observed Textual Facts)

1. **Shared Pre-Pauline / Early Christian Stone *Testimonium* or Hebraizing Greek Revision (Rom 9:33 & 1 Pet 2:6–8):**
   - Both Paul (Rom 9:33) and 1 Peter (2:6–8) agree against the Swete LXX in three independent details: (i) `ἰδοὺ τίθημι ἐν Σιὼν` (instead of LXX `ἐγὼ ἐμβαλῶ εἰς τὰ θεμέλια Σιὼν`), (ii) `πέτρα(ν) σκανδάλου` (instead of LXX `πέτρας πτώματι`), and (iii) stripping LXX Isa 8:14's negation (`οὐχ ὡς ... οὐδὲ ὡς`). Because 1 Peter retains LXX elements Paul omits (`ἀκρογωνιαῖον ἐκλεκτὸν ἔντιμον`, `οὐ μὴ καταισχυνθῇ`) and does not splice Isa 8:14 inside Isa 28:16, direct literary borrowing by 1 Peter from Romans alone is unlikely to explain the full pattern; a shared early Christian stone *testimonium* tradition and/or a Hebraizing Greek text-type circulating in the first century is a strong explanatory hypothesis.
2. **Reason for the LXX Shift in Habakkuk 2:4 and Hebrews' Pronoun Placement:**
   - LXX's switch from 3ms (`נַפְשׁוֹ`, `בֶּאֱמוּנָתוֹ`) to 1cs (`ἡ ψυχή μου`, `ἐκ πίστεώς μου`) may stem from graphic confusion (`ו`/`י`) or theological reading of God as speaker of both halves. Hebrews 10:38 (`ὁ δὲ δίκαιός μου ἐκ πίστεως ζήσεται`) likely reflects either a pre-existing Greek variant (also attested in some LXX witnesses such as Codex Alexandrinus) or an intentional transposition by the author of Hebrews so that `ἐκ πίστεως` remains absolute (as in Paul) while `μου` binds the faithful believer directly to God ("my righteous one").
3. **Why Paul Omitted `μου` in Romans 1:17 and Galatians 3:11:**
   - Whether Paul followed a Greek Vorlage lacking `μου` or deliberately omitted it, leaving `ἐκ πίστεως` unqualified enables his thesis that righteousness is "from faith to faith" (Rom 1:17) and universally open to Gentiles (Gal 3:8–11), rather than restricted to "my [God's] faithfulness" or "his [own] faithfulness."
