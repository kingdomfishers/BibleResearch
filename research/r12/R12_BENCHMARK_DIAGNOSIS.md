## 1. Executive Diagnosis

The 2026-11-19 serial MCP benchmark retest confirmed **full infrastructure stability**: **239/239** responses returned cleanly (**238** `status: "ok"`, **1** `status: "no_match"` on `W025` `"yeast"`, **0** HTTP 503/timeout errors; median latency 1,062 ms, P95 2,617 ms, max 3,719 ms).

However, inspecting the actual top-K ranked results, evidence IDs, epistemic classes, and live `get_connection_evidence_v1` payloads across all 239 cases confirms that **the current discovery and ranking algorithms have major epistemic, compositional, and retrieval defects**:

1. **Cheap Alias Triage Overstates Positive Retrieval (146/215 vs. 98/215 vs. 40/215 clean hits):**
   - While string alias triage flagged **146/215** positives, **58** of those are **superficial keyword/substring matches** (e.g., `dam` inside `a.dam`, or an unrelated grammatical card mentioning `blood`, `Abraham`, or `heart` without connecting the expected passages).
   - Counting only cases where an expected target Strong's ID or expected target biblical witness actually appears inside the case's `top_k`, true target-evidence retrieval is **98/215 (45.6%)** overall—and only **40/215 (18.6%)** are clean, properly classified direct/lexical hits, while **40/215** more are partial/misclassified (`r11:cluster` self-occurrence cards, `r12:path` with overstated epistemic status, or `r12:prop` with synthetic predicates) and **135/215 (62.8%)** miss their expected connection inside `top_k`.
   - The sharpest gap is on **`direct_intertext` (`D001–D055`)**: only **4/55 (7.3%)** surface their target witness in `top_k`, even though **53/55 (96.4%)** of those exact verse pairs already exist directly in the local staged `r10/xrefs/book-*.json.gz` index! Across all 125 passage-seeded positives (`D`+`S`+`W`), **95/125 (76.0%)** already exist as direct `out` or `in` links in `r10/xrefs`, yet runtime discovery never queries `r10/xrefs` on passage-anchored queries.
2. **Confirmed Epistemic & Provenance Negative-Control Failures (10 `CONFIRMED_FAIL_NEG` + 1 `PARTIAL_RISK` across 24 controls):**
   - **`N001–N004`** (`Hagar → ostrich` rank 3, `Eye → angel` rank 5, `Logos → kidney` rank 4, `Covenant → wheel` rank 2) plus **`N021`** (`H1285` generic hub chains) and **`N022`** (`H1904` Genesis 16 merged with 1 Chronicles 14 David's daughters) are **confirmed failures** where chains of disjoint clause witnesses are falsely labeled `documented_lexical_relationship` (Tier 3) with saturated `C = 1.0`.
   - **`N009`** (`"three days resurrection"`): returns **Genesis 5:23** (Enoch's **365 years**, `r11:intersect:verse:H7969:H3117:129`) at **Rank #1** (`score 1090`) as `direct_textual_evidence`.
   - **`N017` & `N023`** (source provenance): `scripts/acquire-r12-external-pilot.mjs` emits fabricated Wikidata predicates (`wd:combustion_fuel_agent`, `wd:related_physical_class`) and synthetic statement GUIDs (`Q897$...:class:Q11426`, `wd-rev-...:fuel:Q93165`) marked `provider: "Wikidata"` and `claim_status: "sourced"`.
   - **`N024`** (structural feature inflation): `r12:struct:H5869:1CH_21:16:2SA_18:24` reports `weighted_jaccard: 1.0` across three encodings (`anchor:H5869`, `participant:H5375`, `role:grammatical_object_of`) of a single lifted-eyes grammatical relation.
   - **`N006`** (`PARTIAL_RISK`): while `r10:homograph:ayin_eye_vs_spring_h5869` is surfaced at #2, `property-bridges.mjs` indexes by `anchor_id` rather than `sense_id`, allowing `H5869` property bridges to conflate sense buckets.
3. **Top-5 Ranking Hijacking:** Across all `239 × 5 = 1,195` top-5 slots in the retest, **819/1,195 (68.5%)** are occupied by just two record types: `r11:gram` (**521 slots = 43.6%**) and `r11:cluster` (**298 slots = 24.9%**), plus `r12:path` (**185 = 15.5%**), while `r11:xref` (0), `r12:prop` (0), `r12:struct` (0), and `r12:orth` (0) receive **zero** top-5 slots across the entire 239-case benchmark. Worse, single common words inside passage queries (e.g., `H7651` "seven", `H3220` "sea", `H1818` "blood", `H6051` "cloud") trigger unrelated `tier 1` curated `explicit-and-typology` records at Rank #1 (`score 1159–1475`) on queries like `Genesis 1:26`, `Genesis 9:13`, `Exodus 12:15`, and `Leviticus 23:15`.

## 4. Ranking Formula & Score Calibration Analysis

### 4.1 Offline Ranker (`r12-ranker-v1`)

Current formula in `src/relationships/path-ranker.ts:620–642, 732–736` and `research/r12/proof-ranker.mjs:24–33, 59–63`:
- `Q = geomMean(quality_weight) * 0.95^(meaningful_hops - 2)`
- `C = min(1, log2(max(1, unique_step_attestation_groups)) / 3)`
- `D = mean(clamp01((feature_idf - 1) / 5))`, `T = min(1, distinct_relation_types / 4)`
- `hub_penalty = 0.10 * mean(min(1, log1p(intermediate_degree) / log1p(MAX)))`
- `interest = clamp01(0.45*Q + 0.30*C + 0.15*D + 0.10*T - hub_penalty)`
