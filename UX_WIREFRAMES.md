Phone: reader, immediately visible word ranges, place actions and event sequence;
inspector/preview accessible through explicit actions. Sheets have reachable
close controls. No stacked modal chains. Comparisons stack source and target.

Missing data: retain source text and labels; show unavailable components and
retry; never substitute a sample for an unavailable live preview. Unknown senses,
unmapped edition text, uncertain places and undated events stay explicit.
