## 3. Backward Discovery Analyses & Root-Cause Clusters

Working backward from every missed discovery, false positive, and investigative bottleneck revealed **7 general root-cause clusters** (none specific to a single verse or word):

1. **Epistemic Overclaiming & Contradictory Provenance**
   - `get_Connection` (`discovery-v2-tools.ts:1954-1965`) had a `|| (t.conn.tier !== undefined && t.conn.tier <= 4)` fallback in `isVerifiedPairwiseCandidate` that elevated incidental proximity/orthographic edges (`r13:wp:H4417:H4428`, `r13:beam:H4417-H4428`) to `direct_link_found: true`.
   - `literary-engine.ts:2791-2811` promoted any 2-consonant proper-noun pair within 10 tokens (`hasGeoParonomasia`) or verse-wide verb cue into `'attested_textual_wordplay'`, causing mechanical false positives (`מְשׁוֹטְטִים↔הַזֵּיתִים`, `עֵינָיִם↔עָוֺן`, `הַשִּׂדִּים↔שְׁתֵּים`, `לְעוֹלָם↔שְׁלֹמֹה`).
   - `r10-discovery.ts:3085-3092` labeled any cluster containing words like `sea`/`border` without verifying sanctuary terms as `temple_and_sanctuary_architecture`, turning Dead Sea (`H4417`) verses into `"Sanctuary and Temple architecture descriptions"` inside `explicit_textual_facts`.
   - `word-model.ts:1026-1173` returned `etymology.status: "unavailable"` whenever `get_word_formation_v1` lacked an entry even when verified STEPBible/HebrewStrong roots existed in `profile.lexical_relations`, while failing to filter out non-etymological `shared_name_base` (`H6160` Arabah under `H4417` Salt).

2. **Homograph, Sense, & Language-Direction Corruption**
   - `discovery-v2-tools.ts:1004-1052` + `word-model.ts:432-477` ignored `tok.lemma_key` when building shared `lexicon` glosses (`H0899="treachery"`, `H1254="to be fat"`, `H6213="to press"`) and leaked inflected token glosses (`"and he showed.me"`) or duplicated entries under `lemma:H*`.
   - `r10-discovery.ts:3321-3330` hardcoded `"Hebrew <source> → Greek <target>"` even when the source lemma was Greek (`G0395`).

3. **Broken Chain Traversal & Unresolvable Evidence IDs**
   - `chain-evidence.ts:71-74` (`chainEvidenceTarget`) picked `evidence.refs[0]` ahead of `evidence.target?.strongs`, causing lexical steps (`H8247 → r11:lex:H8247:0`) to jump to incidental witness verses (`Genesis 43:11`) instead of `H8246`.
   - `discovery-v2-tools.ts:2260-2265` checked `visitedNodes` against `c.refs[0]` instead of `chainEvidenceTarget(c, queryStr)`, filtering out every passage neighbor and leaving `follow_connectionchain` dead-ended (`next_connections: []`) on all passages.
   - `r10-discovery.ts:1073, 6379` emitted doubled `r13:motif:r13:motif:...` IDs that failed `trace_evidence`, and `r13:proof:*` IDs returned `not_found` in `research(evidence)`.

4. **Concept-Lookup Normalization & Disambiguation Blind Spots**
   - `corpus-relationships-rpc.mjs:3805-4007` (`handleResolveFastLookupV1`) failed on standard dotless Hebrew transliterations (`"melek"` vs `"me.lekh"`), failed on Greek oxia/tonos or final-sigma variants (`καρπός`, `λόγος`), and ranked `"thought"` → `H4609` (Temple stairs!) and `"tree"` → `G1186` (18 vv) over `H6086` (`עֵץ`).

5. **Cloudflare 10 ms CPU Crashes & Schema Friction in `research`**
   - `handleDiscoverPatternsV1` and `handleComparePatternsV1` (`corpus-relationships-rpc.mjs:4009-4220`) nested `findConnectedSubgraph` (spawning up to 5 extra discovery passes in one HTTP request), triggering Worker 10 ms CPU aborts (`retryable: true`), and returned errors on zero-match negative controls (`Hagar Ekron forty`) instead of `pattern_count: 0`.
   - `ResearchOpSchema` flattened 20 ops into one schema without aliases (`strongs`, `passages`, `terms`, `query`), and a single invalid op aborted an entire 8-op batch.

6. **Cross-Testament, Cognate, & Structural Comparison Gaps**
   - `passage_compare` (`research-engine.ts:1252-1385`) required a lemma to appear in *all* passages (`every`) and ignored LXX bridges, returning `shared_lemmas: []` for OT+NT comparisons.
   - `compare_patterns` treated multi-chapter passage targets as bag-of-words instead of comparing `literary_structure` templates/deviations (`Gen 1` 6+1 vs `Rev 2–3` 7 uniform).
   - `literary-engine.ts` emitted a false `intensification` artifact on `τάδε` (`G3592`) in `Rev 2–3` and false polarity symmetry on `נְאֻם יְהוָה` in `Isa 55:8`.
   - `op: "lxx"` truncated ranges to verse 1 and returned empty for rare words without verse-level Swete fallback; `op: "numbers"` returned sparse/erroring results; `intersect_words` lacked semantic-family expansion.

7. **Payload Bloat & Latency Spikes**
   - `get_Connection` (`127–265 KB`), `passage_compare` (`up to 1.81 MB`), `occurrences`/`intersect_words` (`315–870 KB`), and `get_Passage` (`up to 839 KB / 16.9 s`) dumped unrequested full token/grammar/occurrence/xref payloads and fired up to 3 parallel discovery requests on ranges.
