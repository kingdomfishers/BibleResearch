# Biblical Context Shards — Data Provenance & Licenses

Generated by `scripts/build-bible-context-shards.mjs` on 2026-11-22.

## Artifacts

- `bible-people-graph.json` — **3,008** biblical human figures and **1,992** deduplicated Scripture-witnessed relationships (1,784 explicit, 208 inferred). Non-human deity rows (`YHVH_1`) are excluded from the human genealogy graph.
- `bible-person-verses.json` — **9,726** canonical verses mapped to **17,682** deduplicated human person attestations (excluding `YHVH_1` and `NA`).
- `bible-places-index.json` — **1,298** biblical places (1,273 with coordinates, 25 unlocated/symbolic with `latitude: null, longitude: null`, 165 matched with Strong's numbers).
